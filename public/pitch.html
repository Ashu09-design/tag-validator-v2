🛡️ The Business Challenge & Solution

For modern enterprises, user tracking data runs marketing budgets and product strategies. Yet, verifying this tracking is manual, costly, and heavily exposed to privacy fines.

⚠️

The Manual Problem

  • Incredibly Slow: QA engineers spend 15-20 mins clearing cookies, clicking popups, and inspecting network requests.
  • Consent Fines: If tracking pixels fire before user agreement, the site breaches GDPR/CCPA, risking legal fines.
  • Data Glitches: Double-fired pageviews or missing click-triggers corrupt analytics data.
✅

The Automated Solution

  • Speed & Scale: Verifies pages in 15 seconds. Handles lists of hundreds of domains simultaneously in the background.
  • Scenario Checks: Automatically verifies tag blocking under 5 distinct consent scenarios.
  • Deep Audit: Simulates natural clicks and hovers to catch user event beacons dynamically.

🚀 Step-by-Step Audit Capabilities

From listing URLs to generating alerts, here is how the validator processes and secures tracking setups:

1

Page Setup (Excel Upload / Crawler)

Provide a direct Excel sheet with test targets, or let the crawler inspect the primary URL and automatically map out all inner landing pages.
2

Multi-Scenario Consent Simulation

Loads each URL in a separate sandboxed session, setting OneTrust cookies or clicking CMP popups. Simulates: Accept All, Reject All, Performance-only, Functional-only, and Targeting-only.
3

Compliance Evaluation

Monitors outgoing network requests at a browser protocol level. If any advertising pixel fires during a "Reject All" session, it is logged and marked as a Compliance failure.
4

Interactions & Click Audits

Dynamically identifies navigation headers, dropdown menus, and custom buttons. Fires trusted browser pointer actions (CDP events) and measures which link calls (Adobe AppMeasurement / GA4 gtag / tealium) fire.
5

SDR (Solution Design Reference) Validation

Compares the events and properties that actually fire against a client-provided specification sheet. Evaluates exact parameter values (e.g. site type, event label) and exports a matching PASS/FAIL sheet.
6

Scheduled Runs & Email Reports

Schedules recurrent jobs (hourly, weekly, etc.) that crawl the latest pages and send emails via Brevo's HTTPS API directly to marketing managers, attaching the complete results.
